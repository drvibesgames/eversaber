Four phases. One clock.

Everything carries through — who you spoke to, where he's standing, what he said and when. Nothing resets between phases.

PHASE 01

The Briefing

Walk round the team's living room. Del runs the job, Jas ran the chats, and Maureen from down t'road knows what she's seen with her own eyes — and what she's only heard at bingo.

  • Case computer with chat logs, social media, timeline and evidence board
  • Reading a record unlocks new questions in the room
  • Wardrobe to change your look before you head out
PHASE 02

The Approach

A drizzly British high street. Customers queue, Kev fries, buses are late. Your man parks his silver estate and runs his routine on the game clock — you just have to be there to see it.

  • Note his arrival time, check the plate, talk to the locals
  • Stare, follow or crowd him and he'll get suspicious
  • Push it too far and he's gone — he will not wait for you
PHASE 03

The Sting

Text-driven, ten seconds a choice, with the world still running behind you. Catch his contradictions, keep your temper, and decide when to ring it in.

  • Police are twenty seconds out once you call — keep him talking
  • Shouting and grabbing only get you a runner, or worse
  • Watch the police physically arrive and walk over
PHASE 04

The Call

A cinematic comms channel: live speaker panels, a radio waveform, and a transcript that types out as they talk. Report what you know — and only what you know.

  • Hand over evidence strongest-first
  • Overstate anything and it comes back to bite you
  • Endings run from arrest to "not enough evidence on him"

Controls

Keyboard

  • WASD / arrows — move
  • E — talk, use, chat to the suspect
  • F — start the sting (stand next to him)
  • C — phone: Del, police, evidence
  • 1–9 — pick a response
  • Esc — pause · M — mute

Touch

  • Joystick — move
  • USE — talk and interact
  • STING — lights up when you're next to him
  • PHONE — Del, police, evidence
  • Tap a response to answer
