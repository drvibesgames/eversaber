A shift on the ward

Rescue. Treat. Feed. Return.

Every casualty runs on two clocks: one while they wait in the dirt, a fresh one once they are in your care.

01

Rescue

Soldiers fall in the battle behind you, then crawl out of one of three tunnels. Grab a stretcher and haul them in before their timer runs dry. Ward full? Set the stretcher down and come back.

02

Treat

Lay them on a bed frame. A bubble shows what they need next. Nanogel comes straight from the med cabinet.

03

Feed

Cook Protein Packs into Hot Stew, pick Starberries off the bushes outside, brew Glow Broth from Glowshrooms. Pull food off the stove before it burns.

04

Return

Healed troops march back into the tunnels and you can watch them rejoin the firefight in the background. Every save earns credits.

One button does the medicine.
