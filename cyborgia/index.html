How a run works · 遊び方

Drive. Earn. Rebuild.

Every attempt pays out, even the ones that end in a ditch. Each run gets you a little further down the road.

  1. 1

    Floor it

    Hold the gas and keep your wheels down. Tilt in the air to land jumps. When the tank runs dry, the run is over.

  2. 2

    Bank points

    Every metre and every cyborg zombie destroyed earns points. Later stages pay more for both.

  3. 3

    Upgrade

    Spend points on armour, weapons, fuel tanks and front-end defences, then go again. Reach the goal gate to unlock the next car.

The garage · 整備場

Bolt on something worse

Every car has its own upgrade path. Armour plates appear on the car as you buy them, and every weapon and defence changes how the car looks and fights.

  • Armour装甲

    More hull, harder ramming, less slowdown when you hit the horde.

  • Weapon武装

    A roof turret that aims itself. Clears leapers off your roof.

  • Fuel燃料

    Bigger tanks mean longer runs. Four times the throttle time at full.

  • Defence防御

    Spikes, saws, flails and plasma blades on the nose.
